## 1. Client Proposal Generator
Prompt:
Act as an expert freelance proposal writer with 10+ years experience. Write a professional, persuasive proposal for a [industry] client requesting [service]. Include: problem understanding, solution overview, timeline (3 phases), pricing tiers (basic/pro/premium), social proof placeholders, and a strong CTA. Tone: confident, value-focused, no jargon. Length: 800-1200 words. Client details: [paste].

## 2. Content Calendar for Personal Brand
Prompt:
Create a 30-day content calendar for a [niche] freelancer on Xiaohongshu/Instagram/TikTok. For each day: topic, hook (first 3 seconds), key points, CTA, hashtag suggestions. Focus on building authority and attracting high-ticket clients. Mix educational, behind-scenes, and offer posts. Output in table format.

## 3. Pricing Strategy Calculator Prompt
Prompt:
Help me price my [service] as a freelancer. Inputs: my target monthly income [X], hours available [Y], market rate research [Z], value delivered (time saved/money made for client). Calculate: hourly rate, project rate, package prices. Suggest 3 tiers with clear deliverables. Explain psychology of pricing. Flag risks of underpricing.

## 4. Cold Outreach Sequence (Email/WeChat)
Prompt:
Write a 5-message cold outreach sequence for [ideal client type] offering [service]. Sequence: value-first intro, case study teaser, social proof, soft offer, final follow-up. Each message under 100 words for WeChat or email. Personalization placeholders. Compliance: no spam, value-driven.

## 5. AI Workflow for Service Delivery
Prompt:
Design a step-by-step AI-assisted workflow for delivering [service] (e.g., copywriting, design briefs, research reports). Tools: Claude/ChatGPT, specific prompts for each step, quality checks, human review points. Goal: reduce delivery time by 60% while maintaining quality. Output as numbered process with example prompts.

## 6. Niche Validation Research Prompt
Prompt:
I want to validate the market for [idea] in [market: China/global]. Research: search volume signals, competitor analysis (top 5), customer pain points from forums (Reddit/Zhihu/Xiaohongshu style), willingness to pay estimates, differentiation opportunities. Provide actionable go/no-go criteria.

## 7. Weekly Business Review Prompt
Prompt:
Conduct a weekly business review for my freelance practice. Analyze: revenue this week vs target, pipeline, top wins/losses, content performance, next week's priorities. Ask clarifying questions if data incomplete. Output: metrics summary, 3 key insights, action plan for next 7 days.

## 8. Testimonial Request Script
Prompt:
Write a polite, effective request for a testimonial after project completion. Include: specific questions to ask client, thank you message, how to use the testimonial (website, Xiaohongshu, proposals). Offer to draft it based on their feedback for easy approval.

## 9. Upsell Sequence Prompt
Prompt:
Create a post-project upsell sequence for clients who bought [service]. Identify natural next services, timing, value messaging. 3 touchpoints: immediate thank you + soft offer, 7-day value add, 30-day check-in. Keep it helpful, not pushy.

## 10. Personal Brand Positioning Statement
Prompt:
Help me craft a clear positioning statement for my freelance brand. Inputs: my skills [list], target clients [who], unique angle [what], results I deliver [outcomes]. Output: 1-sentence tagline, 3-sentence bio, elevator pitch (30 seconds), and differentiation from generalists.
